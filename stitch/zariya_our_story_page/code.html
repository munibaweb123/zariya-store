Our Story

Crafting heritage for the modern woman.

The Visionaries

Founded by two lifelong friends with a shared passion for the intricate artistry of Pakistan, ZARIYA began as a dream to bridge the gap between ancient craftsmanship and the contemporary wardrobe.

Growing up amidst the vibrant textile markets and the rhythmic hum of handlooms, we saw the incredible talent of local artisans fading. We built ZARIYA to preserve these stories—to ensure that every hand-embroidered motif and every hand-spun thread continues to find a home with the discerning modern woman.

Our journey is one of collaboration, respect, and enduring beauty. We believe that what you wear should be a testament to your roots, reimagined for the path you lead today.
